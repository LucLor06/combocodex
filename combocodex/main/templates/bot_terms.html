1. About the Bot

The ComboCodex Discord bot is another way to use ComboCodex without leaving Discord. It lets you search for Brawlhalla team combos, share results, and even submit your own combos directly through Discord.

By using the bot, you agree to these Terms of Service, Discord's terms and policies, and any applicable ComboCodex rules.

2. What Can the Bot Do?

You can use the bot to search for combos and get links to results from ComboCodex. This works in any Discord server where the bot is installed and has permission to respond.

Some features, like linking your ComboCodex account and submitting new combos, are only available in the official ComboCodex Discord server.

3. Linking Your Account

To submit combos through Discord, you'll need to link your Discord account to your ComboCodex account. This allows the bot to recognize your account and submit combos on your behalf.

You can unlink your Discord account through ComboCodex at any time. You're responsible for keeping your account secure and making sure nobody else uses it without your permission.

4. Uploading and Sharing Combos

When you submit a combo through the bot, it's uploaded to ComboCodex and may be publicly displayed on the website. This includes any videos or other information you provide with your submission.

By uploading content, you confirm that you have the right to share it and give ComboCodex permission to store and display it as part of the website.

Please don't upload anything illegal, malicious, stolen, or otherwise inappropriate. You're responsible for the content you submit, and you can delete your own combos through ComboCodex, including their associated videos.
